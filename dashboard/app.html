How it works

1. Both pools emit Sync on every trade. The reactive contract on Lasna mirrors both.
2. When the gap beats fees, it sizes the optimal trade in closed form and fires two callbacks in one tx: buy on the cheap chain, sell on the rich one.
3. Executors re-quote, enforce slippage + timestamp deadlines, swap, and report back.
4. The brain books PnL, unlocks, and re-checks the gap. No bots, no keepers.
